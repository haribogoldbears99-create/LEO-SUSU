Leo Math Quest Leo Math Quest: Final Design Documentation This document outlines the comprehensive pedagogical framework, mechanical systems, and content structure for Leo Math Quest, aligned with Cambridge Primary IGCSE standards. Game Mechanics and Interactive Design DragonBox Tactile Mechanics The core interaction layer utilizes "DragonBox" tactile mechanics. Students manipulate mathematical elements directly on the screen. Instead of traditional multiple-choice inputs, players drag, drop, and group icons to solve algebraic and arithmetic expressions. This allows for a deep, intuitive understanding of balance and equivalence before moving to abstract notation. Prodigy Canvas Battle Animations Mathematical engagement is framed through canvas-based battle animations. When a student encounters a challenge, the screen shifts to a turn-based encounter. Correct answers trigger dynamic animations where Leo uses elemental abilities to clear obstacles. Every mathematical operation is visualized as an action, reinforcing the cause-and-effect relationship of logic and outcome. MathTango Eye-Rest Island To promote digital wellness and sustained focus, every question is followed by a visit to the MathTango Eye-Rest Island. This serves as a micro-reward system where players can engage in low-intensity interactions, such as placing a new creature or expanding their island habitat. This loop ensures the cognitive load is managed effectively, preventing student burnout. LINE Parent Report and Scholarship The game integrates directly with the LINE messaging platform. Upon completion of milestones or daily sessions, parents receive a detailed "Progress Snapshot." This report includes the student's mastery levels across Cambridge Stages. As a tangible incentive, reaching significant milestones triggers a 500 THB Scholarship Voucher, which is delivered directly to the parent via the LINE interface. Cambridge Primary Curriculum: Question Bank Ruby World: Stage 1 (ป.1) Focus: Number sense, basic addition/subtraction, and 2D shapes. DragonBox Interaction: Drag the stars into two boxes to show that 5 is made of 2 and 3. Logic Quest: What is the next number in the pattern? 2, 4, 6, 8, ... Shape Sorter: Identify the triangle among a circle, square, and rectangle. Counting Challenge: Count the apples. If Leo eats 2 out of 7, how many remain? Comparison: Which number is greater: 14 or 41? Time Discovery: Which hand on the clock shows the hour? Basic Ops: Solve 10 + 5 using the tactile number line. Pattern Mastery: Fill in the blank: Blue, Red, Blue, __, Blue. Weight Discovery: Drag the heavier item to the low side of the scale. Number Bonds: Find the missing partner for 10: 7 + __ = 10. Sapphire World: Stage 2 (ป.2) Focus: Place value, measuring length, and simple multiplication. Place Value: Build the number 45 using 10-blocks and 1-cubes. Measurement: Use the virtual ruler to measure the dragon's tail in centimeters. Multiplication Intro: Create 3 groups of 5 stars. How many stars in total? Division Intro: Share 12 gems equally between 2 treasure chests. Geometry: How many sides does a pentagon have? Data Handling: Look at the tally chart. Which fruit is the most popular? Ordinal Numbers: Which position is the gold trophy in: 1st, 2nd, or 3rd? Subtraction: Solve 50 - 25 using the "jump back" method. Fraction Intro: Drag the line to cut the pizza into two equal halves. Estimation: Estimate if a pencil is closer to 10cm or 100cm. Emerald World: Stage 3 (ป.3) Focus: 3-digit numbers, formal multiplication, and unit conversion. Place Value: What is the value of the digit 7 in 742? Advanced Addition: Calculate 156 + 234 using the column method. Multiplication: Solve 4 x 8. Show your work on the canvas. Division: What is 21 divided by 3? Time Management: If a movie starts at 2:00 PM and lasts 90 minutes, when does it end? Money Math: You have 100 THB. You buy a snack for 45 THB. How much change do you get? Fractions: Which is larger: 1/4 or 1/2? Mass: Convert 1 kilogram into grams. 3D Shapes: How many faces does a cube have? Perimeter: Calculate the perimeter of a square with sides of 5cm. Amethyst World: Stage 4 (ป.4) Focus: Decimals, area, complex data, and improper fractions. Decimals: Write "five tenths" as a decimal number. Area Quest: Find the area of a rectangle with length 6m and width 4m. Negative Numbers: What is 5 degrees colder than 2 degrees Celsius? Advanced Fractions: Convert the improper fraction 5/2 into a mixed number. Data Analysis: Calculate the mode of this set: 2, 4, 4, 6, 8. Rounding: Round 1,456 to the nearest hundred. Angle Identification: Is a 120-degree angle acute, right, or obtuse? Equivalent Fractions: Find a fraction equivalent to 2/3. Symmetry: Drag the line of symmetry across the butterfly image. Multi-step Problem: Leo buys 3 potions for 15 gold each and pays with a 50-gold coin. How much change?
